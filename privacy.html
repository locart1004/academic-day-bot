Privacy Policy — Academic Day Bot

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Academic Day Bot is an internal tool for the J-EMRTP Western emergency medicine residency program. This page explains what data it uses.

Google account data

The app connects to one Google account, owned by the program's chief resident. It uses Google Drive access only to create folders and upload lecture slide files into the program's shared lecture library. It does not read, change, or delete any other files, and it does not access Gmail, contacts, or calendar.

Resident data

The app stores residents' names, resident IDs, lecture assignments, and submitted slide files so it can schedule lectures and send reminders.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties. Data is used only to run the residency program's academic day.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The account owner can remove the app's access at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact

For questions, contact the chief residents of the J-EMRTP Western program.
